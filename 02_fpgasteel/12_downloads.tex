\chapter{Downloads}
\label{chap:fpgasteel-downloads}

A copy of all of them, plus a prebuilt \term{SD} card image with every |sw/| example already compiled onto it (see the project's Quick Start\footnote{See Section~\ref{sec:fpgasteel-quick-start}}), is also mirrored here, for convenience:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Shared folder, linked from the \texttt{FPGAdocs} repository's \texttt{README}\footnote{\texttt{FPGAdocs} \texttt{README} --- \url{https://github.com/tceccarini/FPGAdocs/blob/main/README.md}} (publicly accessible, no account required).
\end{itemize}
