\chapter{Downloads}
\label{chap:fpgasteel-downloads}

\gterm{quartusprime}{Quartus Prime} 22.1 \cite{ALTERA-QUARTUS}, the \term{ARM} toolchain (|xPack Arm Embedded GCC|) \cite{XPACK-ARM-GCC}, |xPack OpenOCD| \cite{XPACK-OPENOCD}, \term{Eclipse} \term{IDE} for Embedded C/C++ \cite{ECLIPSE-EMBED-CDT}, and the \term{Ubuntu 22.04 LTS} image \cite{UBUNTU-2204} used by \gterm{fpgasteel}{FPGAsteel} are all available from their respective official sites.

A shared folder, linked from the \texttt{FPGAdocs} repository's \texttt{README}\footnote{\texttt{FPGAdocs} \texttt{README} --- \url{https://github.com/tceccarini/FPGAdocs/blob/main/README.md}} (publicly accessible, no account required), mirrors everything needed to follow this tutorial without hunting down each piece separately:

\begin{itemize}
    \item the compiled thesis \term{PDF};
    \item the camera adapter board documentation (schematics, bill of materials, \gterm{pcb}{PCB} layouts);
    \item third-party reference documentation (datasheets and manuals cited throughout this work);
    \item installers for the software listed above;
    \item a prebuilt \term{SD} card image with every \repopath{sw/} example already compiled onto it (see the project's Quick Start\footnote{See Section~\ref{sec:fpgasteel-quick-start}}).
\end{itemize}
